Orbmancer - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Русская версия

This policy explains how the mobile game Orbmancer (the "game", application id com.orbmancer.game) handles information. The game is made by LunguGames ("we", "us").

Summary

The game does not collect, store on our side, or share any personal information. It does not connect to the internet: it does not request the Android permission for network access.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following only on your device, so that you can continue where you left off:

  • your progress (coins, gems, heroes, talents, chapters, missions, daily rewards and records);
  • an unfinished run, so it can be resumed;
  • your settings (language, music and sound volume, vibration, reduced motion, aiming mode).

This information never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

If the backup feature of your Android device is turned on, Android may include the game's saved data in your device backup in your Google account. This backup is handled by Google under your device's backup settings; we do not receive it.

Permissions

The game uses the vibration permission only to make the phone vibrate during play (you can turn vibration off in the game's settings). It requests no other permission.

Advertising and purchases

This version of the game contains no advertising or payment software from other companies, so no information is passed to such services. Advertising and in-app purchases through Google Play are planned for a later version; this policy will be updated before that version is released and will then describe what information is involved.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.
