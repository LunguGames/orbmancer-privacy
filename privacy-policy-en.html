Orbmancer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Русская версия

This policy explains how the mobile game Orbmancer ("the game", application id com.orbmancer.game) handles information. The game is developed by LunguGames ("we").

Summary

  • Your game progress is stored on your device. The game has no accounts.
  • The game shows ads from the Yandex Advertising Network. The Yandex Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects some device data to serve and measure ads (details below).
  • The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports to AppMetrica (Yandex).

What is stored on your device

Only on your device the game stores your progress (coins, gems, heroes, talents, chapters, quests, rewards and records), an unfinished run and your settings. This data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. If device backup is on, Android may keep a copy in your Google account, managed by Google.

Ads (Yandex Advertising Network)

The game has optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch a video for an in-game reward), and a full-screen ad may appear between runs. Ads are provided by Yandex LLC. When an ad is requested or shown, the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (including the AppMetrica SDK it contains) collects and sends to Yandex:

  • the device's IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, carrier);
  • ad interactions: impressions, clicks, views;
  • diagnostic data about the ad SDK.

Yandex uses this data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under its Privacy Policy and the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK terms. The game does not pass location data to the SDK.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game tells Yandex that there is no consent to personalised ads, so ads there are not personalised. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on the device).

Analytics (AppMetrica)

To understand how the game is played and to fix errors, the game uses AppMetrica, an analytics service of Yandex LLC. The AppMetrica SDK receives: game events (for example, a run started and its result, a chapter cleared, an ad watched, with no names or contact details), device and app information (model, OS and game version, language, the store the game was installed from), device identifiers (the Android advertising ID, AppMetrica's installation ID), the IP address (from which an approximate country and city can be derived) and crash reports. Location tracking is off. Yandex processes this data under its Privacy Policy and the AppMetrica SDK terms; we see only anonymous aggregated statistics.

RuStore version

The version of the game from RuStore contains the RuStore SDK (VK LLC): it shows the app rating form and offers game updates. To do this the SDK talks to the RuStore app on your device; the data is processed under RuStore's documents. The Google Play version does not contain this SDK.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): lets the ad SDK read the advertising ID.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback during play (can be turned off in the game's settings).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

We do not store personal data about you ourselves. For data collected by Yandex for ads and analytics, use the tools and contacts given in its policy. For privacy questions about this game, write to us and we will answer.
